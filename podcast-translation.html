How to translate a podcast episode

  1. Upload the audio file — or the video version if you publish one.
  2. Choose the target language and request the transcript, the dub, or both.
  3. Confirm the per-minute quote — shown before payment, valid 30 minutes.
  4. Download the SRT and MP3; publish the dub as a separate feed or a bonus episode.

Why audio-only podcasts translate better than video

  • No on-screen text to re-render — nothing has to be re-typeset.
  • No lip-sync expectation — listeners accept a different voice far more readily than viewers accept mismatched mouths.
  • The transcript is the product — show notes and blog posts are straightforward once you have the text.
  • Interview audio is the main risk — crosstalk and phone-quality guests degrade transcription, not the language model.

Frequently asked questions

Can I upload audio only, without video?

Yes. Podcast files are accepted directly; there is no video frame to render.

Do I get a transcript I can publish?

Yes. The SRT output contains the translated text with timecodes; strip the timecodes to get a plain transcript.

Can I dub only part of an episode?

Upload the segment you want dubbed; pricing is per minute.

Is there a free trial?

60 seconds and 100 MB, subtitles only, no account, limited per network address.

Will the dubbed voice sound like the host?

No. The dub uses a synthetic voice in the target language; voice cloning is not offered.
