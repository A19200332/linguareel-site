Spanish is not one language

Machine output lands closest to neutral Latin American Spanish. Before publishing, decide three things:

  • Spain or Latin America — vocabulary differs, and Spain uses "vosotros".
  • Formality — "tú" for consumer products, "usted" for B2B and training.
  • Numbers — 1,000.50 in one convention, 1.000,50 in the other.

A native review of those three points costs less than re-recording a dub.

What improves the Spanish transcript before upload

  • Remove background music that sits over speech.
  • Keep one speaker per clip where possible.
  • Say product and brand names clearly, and list them for the reviewer.
  • Trim intros and long silences — pricing is per minute.
