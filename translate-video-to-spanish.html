Spanish is not one language

Machine output lands closest to neutral Latin American Spanish. Before publishing, decide:

  • Spain vs Latin America — vocabulary and the "vosotros" form differ.
  • Formality — "tú" for consumer products, "usted" for B2B and training.
  • Numbers and currency — 1,000.50 vs 1.000,50.

A native pass on these three points is cheaper than re-recording a dub.

What makes the transcript better

  • Remove background music over speech before uploading.
  • Keep one speaker per clip where possible.
  • Say product names clearly, and write them down for the reviewer.
  • Trim intros and long silences — pricing is per minute.
