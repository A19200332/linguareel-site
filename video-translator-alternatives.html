Cost comparison, without fake numbers

Do not trust "free forever" claims on either side. Compare three numbers for your own clip:

  1. Total cost of a 5-minute video in your target language, all taxes included.
  2. Time to first usable draft — hours, not marketing claims.
  3. Cost of the second revision after a native speaker finds a wrong name.
