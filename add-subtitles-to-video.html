Why automatic subtitles usually look wrong

  • Line length. More than ~42 characters per line forces the eye to jump; auto-captions often ignore this.
  • Reading speed. Subtitles should be readable in the time they are on screen; long lines at 3 seconds are unreadable.
  • Names. A misheard product name propagates to every line it appears in.
  • Translation drift. If subtitles and a dub come from two different passes, they contradict each other.

The SRT is editable on purpose: if you add subtitles to video with machine timing plus a five-minute human pass, you get a better result than a fully manual transcription.

Why adding subtitles to a video helps search

Subtitle text is indexed. A video with an accurate English SRT is discoverable by search; the same video with a translated SRT is discoverable in that language too. That is a distribution argument for subtitling even when viewers watch with sound on.
