Four things that break in Arabic video translation

  • Text direction. Latin brand names inside Arabic lines reorder punctuation; rendering subtitles and the dub from one pipeline avoids manual bidi fixes.
  • Numerals. Pick Western (1, 2, 3) or Eastern Arabic (١, ٢, ٣) digits and stay consistent.
  • Dialect. MSA is understood everywhere; Gulf or Egyptian dialect feels local but narrows the audience. Default to MSA for product and training content.
  • Fonts. Many players ship without an Arabic font; a burned-in MP4 sidesteps the problem.

Upload checklist for better Arabic output

  • Strip background music that sits over the speech.
  • Keep one speaker per clip if possible.
  • List brand names that must not be translated.
  • Trim long intros — pricing is per minute.
