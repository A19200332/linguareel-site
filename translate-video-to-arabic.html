Four things that break in Arabic video translation

  • Text direction. Mixing Latin product names inside Arabic lines reorders punctuation. Burned-in subtitles from a single pipeline avoid manual bidi fixes.
  • Numerals. Decide between Western (1, 2, 3) and Eastern Arabic (佟, 佗, 伲) digits and keep it consistent.
  • Dialect. Modern Standard Arabic is understood everywhere; Gulf or Egyptian dialect feels local but narrows the audience. Default to MSA for training and product content.
  • Fonts. Many players ship without a proper Arabic font. A subtitled MP4 side-steps the issue entirely.

Which regions this reaches

Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Egypt, Kuwait, Qatar, Jordan, Morocco, Algeria, Iraq and the wider diaspora. If you sell to one of them, MSA plus local currency and dates is the safe default.
