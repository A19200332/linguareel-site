How to dub a video with AI

  1. Upload the source video or audio — MP4, MOV, MKV, AVI, WEBM, or an audio-only file.
  2. Pick the target language and outputs — dub only, or dub plus subtitles.
  3. Confirm the per-minute quote — visible before payment, valid 30 minutes.
  4. Download the MP3 and MP4 — the dub and the burned-in subtitles share wording.

What AI dubbing cannot do (be honest with your audience)

  • It does not re-animate lips. Lip-sync dubbing is a different, more expensive service.
  • It does not clone your voice by default. Expect a synthetic voice in the target language.
  • It does not fix bad audio. Music over speech degrades the transcript, and everything downstream inherits that.
  • It does not replace a native reviewer for jokes, legal wording or brand names.
