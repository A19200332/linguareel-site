Frequently asked questions

Is the AI video translator free?

The trial is free: 60 seconds and 100 MB, subtitles only, no account. Full videos are priced per minute, quoted before payment.

Does it dub the video as well as subtitling it?

Yes. You can request an MP3 dub, an SRT subtitle file, or both, all rendered from the same transcript.

Can I translate an audio-only file?

Yes. Podcast and voice-only uploads are accepted and can return a dubbed MP3.

Which languages are supported?

12+ target languages, selected after upload. Changing the language re-quotes the job.

Who owns the uploaded video?

You do. LinguaReel receives a limited licence to store, process and return the file, and you must own the rights to translate it.

Why is the subtitle and the dub wording identical?

Because both are rendered from one transcript instead of two independent translation passes.
